Quiet Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Applies to Quiet Keyboard (package com.quiet.keyboard). Effective October 24, 2026.

Quiet Keyboard works offline. Nothing you type leaves your phone.
The app has no internet permission, so Android blocks it from sending anything anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

What Quiet Keyboard collects

Nothing. It does not collect, sell or share any personal or device data, and the developer never receives any information about you, what you type or how you use the app.

About Android's keyboard warning

When you turn on any keyboard, Android warns that it may be able to collect what you type, including passwords and card numbers. That warning is shown for every keyboard. Quiet Keyboard processes what you write on your phone only and has no way to send it anywhere.

Nothing is learned in password fields, in apps' incognito modes, or anywhere an app asks keyboards not to learn.

Backups

Cloud backup is turned off, so nothing goes to Google's servers. When you move to a new phone by cable or direct Wi-Fi during setup, Android can copy your keyboard options straight to the new phone. Handwriting samples and learned words are never copied or backed up. Uninstalling Quiet Keyboard deletes everything.

Children

Quiet Keyboard collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Quiet Keyboard will never add data collection without saying so here first.
