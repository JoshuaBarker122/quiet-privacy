Quiet — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Quiet launcher (package com.quiet.launcher). Effective October 25, 2026. Quiet Keyboard is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

Quiet works offline. Nothing leaves your phone.
Quiet has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

What Quiet collects

Nothing. Quiet does not collect, sell or share any personal or device data. The developer never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

What Quiet uses on your phone

To work as a home screen, Quiet reads some information on your phone only. None of it is sent anywhere.

WhatWhyWhen
Your list of installed apps and their shortcutsTo show, search and open your appsAlways (a home screen needs it)
Calendar eventsTo show today's agendaOnly if you allow calendar access. Events are read when shown and not stored.
App usage (which apps you used recently)To show “Recent” appsOnly if you grant Usage access in Android settings
NotificationsTo show a dot, a count or message bubbles on an appOnly if you grant Notification access. Quiet counts notifications per app and never reads what messages say. With Bubbles on, it reads the sender's name and picture of message notifications to show who messaged, in memory only: never stored or sent, and dropped when the notification goes or you change the setting.
A contact's name and phone numberFor a gesture or pattern that messages or calls that personOnly for a contact you pick in Android's contact picker. Quiet has no access to your other contacts.
Icon packsTo draw app icons from a pack you installedOnly if you choose an icon pack
Theme filesTo import or export a themeOnly files you choose

Where your settings are kept

Your favorites, folders, themes, and Orbit circles and patterns are stored in Quiet's private storage on your phone. Quiet turns off Android's cloud backup, so none of it goes to Google's servers. When you move to a new phone by cable or direct Wi-Fi during setup, Android can copy these settings straight to the new phone. Uninstalling Quiet deletes everything.

Other apps

Some buttons open other apps: “Get icon packs” opens your app store, links open your browser, and shortcuts open the apps you chose. What those apps do is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

Quiet collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Quiet will never add data collection without saying so here first.
